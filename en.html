How it works

No single check finds everything. Peepless combines several methods – and guides you through the room step by step.

Wi-Fi check

Finds devices on the accommodation network that behave like security cameras – with device name, manufacturer and video services.

Bluetooth & trackers

Detects AirTags, Tile, SmartTag and other trackers, plus cheap cameras with typical names. With a hotter-colder finder.

Camera check

The flashlight reveals lens reflections. A motion check filters out glare, and on-device AI marks camera-like shapes.

Guided room check

Bathroom, bed, desk, sockets: Peepless walks you through all typical hiding spots. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth run automatically.

PDF report

Every check ends with a clear report – what was checked, what stood out and what to do next. For the hotel, host or police.

On-the-move watch

Tells you when the same tracker follows you to several places.

Honest about limits

No app can find every camera. Cameras that are switched off, well hidden or only use mobile data often stay invisible. Peepless shows indications instead of false certainty – and explains what each result means and what the app cannot check.

FAQ

How much does Peepless cost?

A one-time purchase (€4.99 in the eurozone, local prices elsewhere). No subscription, no in-app purchases and no paywall before results.

Do I need internet?

No. All checks run offline on your device. For the Wi-Fi check you only need to be connected to the accommodation's Wi-Fi.

Does Peepless find every hidden camera?

No – and no serious app can promise that. Peepless combines several checks to improve your chances and tells you openly what it cannot detect.

What should I do if I find something?

Don't destroy the device, take photos, save the report and inform the hotel or host. If you have a well-founded suspicion, contact the police. The app includes templates and an overview of the legal situation.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone (iOS) and Android phones. The app is available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Turkish, Polish and Arabic.
